# 5 · Printing and assembly

All printable parts are generated by `python3 cad/build_parts.py` → `cad/stl/*.stl` (already in print orientation, watertight). The script is parametric: change `design/params.py` (leg lengths, wheel size, body size) or the interface constants at the top of `cad/build_parts.py` (motor bolt circles, bearing size, magnets) and re‑run. `cad/assembly_nominal_pose.stl` is the whole robot standing, for a quick look in any slicer/viewer.

> **Check before printing**: the motor bolt patterns (`HIP_PCD`, `HIP_STATOR_PCD`, `WHEEL_STATOR_PCD`, `WHEEL_ROTOR_PCD`) against the drawings of the motors you received.

Printer: 256 × 256 mm bed (Bambu P1S/X1, Prusa Core One, Voron 250 …), hardened nozzle for CF filaments. 0.2 mm layers unless noted.

## 5.2 Hardware prep
* Heat‑set inserts (M3): floor plate and top frame side edges (8 each), top frame roof‑rack (4), shell magnet bosses → magnets instead (glue, check polarity so front/rear shells attract the belly pan).
* Press 608‑2RS bearings into both shins (knee end) and the front shin foot end; the M8 shoulder bolt is the pin.

## 5.3 Assembly order
1. **Chassis box**: bolt the 4 hip motors (DM‑J4310) to the inner faces of the side plates (stator bolt circle), output through the plate. Join the side plates with the floor plate and top frame (M3 into inserts). The hip motor outputs now stick out on both sides.
2. **Legs**: thighs onto the motor output flanges (4 × M3, counter‑bored). Front shin on the front thigh knee pin, rear shin on the rear thigh knee pin; the front shin's foot ring slides over the rear shin's axle boss (the two shins share the wheel axis). Bolt the MF9025 stator to the rear shin foot (M4), then the wheel rim onto the motor's output face (6 × M3), then stretch the TPU tyre over the rim lips.
3. **Wiring**: PDB + e‑stop + fuses in the belly area beside the battery rails; motor XT30s and CAN daisy chains along the side plates; CAN termination at the last motor of each bus.
4. **Electronics sled**: Pi 5 (grommets) on the front face, Teensy carrier (IMU hard‑mounted) + bucks on the back face; cameras and GPS run to the sled connector. Slide the sled into the top‑frame guides, plug the 12‑pin + XT30, clamp with the two thumb screws.
5. **Battery tray**: strap the 6S pack, slide in from the rear until the latch clicks.
6. **Sensors**: sonars into the face (mouth + cheeks) and rear brackets, Sharp sensors in the chin, cameras on their tilted mounts behind the forehead/rear holes, eyes (WS2812 rings) behind the visor.
7. **Shells**: belly pan, then front and rear shells (magnets), bumpers (TPU, M3 into the shell bosses), payload bay + lid, electronics cap with the GPS puck.
8. **Calibrate** (§3.8 of [03_electronics_and_settings.md](03_electronics_and_settings.md)): fold the legs onto the end‑stop pegs → `bolt_cli.py calibrate`.

## 5.4 Mechanical checks
* Legs must move freely over the whole range by hand (motors unpowered), thighs must not touch the shell, the end‑stop pegs touch the thighs only in the fully folded pose.
* Wheels: < 0.5 mm wobble; the rim must not touch the motor body (1.5 mm radial gap).
* Total mass on a scale ≈ 6.4 kg without payload. If very different, update the component masses in `design/params.py` and re‑run `python3 sim/lqr_design.py` (gains change with mass!).
